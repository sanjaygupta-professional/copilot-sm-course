# AI-Assisted Reflective Journal for Scrum Masters

**Scrum Master:** You (Velocity Squad)
**Purpose:** Daily micro-reflections with weekly AI-powered pattern analysis

---

## How to Use This Journal

1. **Daily (5 minutes):** Fill in the day's entry — keep it brief and honest
2. **Weekly (15 minutes):** Paste the week's entries into Copilot with the analysis prompt
3. **Monthly (30 minutes):** Review 4 weeks of analysis for deeper patterns

The power of this journal is in the accumulation. One day tells you nothing. One week shows tendencies. One month reveals your coaching identity.

---

## Example Week — Sprint 47, Week 1

### Monday, October 14

**What Happened:**
- SoS meeting: reported David availability concern to Ana and Chris; no resolution offered
- 1:1 with Marcus — he opened up about feeling overshadowed by Lisa in planning
- Prepped Sprint Review materials for tomorrow
- Raj asked me to help him respond to Richard's email about adding scope mid-sprint
- Helped Emma understand the Definition of Done for the notification stories

**Energy Level:** 3

**Best Moment:** Marcus actually talked to me about his concerns. I think he's starting to trust me.

**Hardest Moment:** The Raj/Richard scope creep situation. I drafted a pushback email with Raj but he was nervous to send it. I felt like I was doing his job for him.

**What I Did Well:**
- Created a safe space for Marcus — asked open questions, didn't rush to advice
- Caught the scope creep early before it hit the team

**What I'd Do Differently:**
- With Raj, I should have coached him to write the email himself instead of drafting it for him. I enabled dependency.

**What I'm Noticing About Myself as SM:**
I default to solving problems for people instead of helping them solve their own.

## Weekly Pattern Analysis Prompt

At the end of each week, paste all 5 daily entries into Copilot with this prompt:

```
Here are my daily SM reflective journal entries for this week:

[Paste all 5 entries]

Analyze these entries and tell me:

1. ENERGY PATTERNS
   - What types of activities drained me vs. energized me?
   - Is there a day-of-week pattern?

2. RECURRING THEMES
   - What issues or challenges appeared more than once?
   - What am I repeatedly saying I'd "do differently" but haven't changed yet?

3. COACHING IDENTITY
   - Based on my "What I'm Noticing" entries, what's my emerging SM identity?
   - What are my top 2 strengths and top 2 growth areas this week?

4. AVOIDANCE PATTERNS
   - What am I consistently avoiding or postponing?
   - What confrontations or difficult conversations am I deferring?

5. ACTION RECOMMENDATIONS
   - Based on this week's patterns, what are the 3 highest-leverage
     changes I could make next week?
   - Be specific and actionable — not "communicate better" but
     "prepare a 1-page velocity dashboard before your Thursday 1:1
     with Jennifer."

6. GROWTH TRAJECTORY
   - Compared to the previous week's analysis (if available), am I
     improving in the areas I identified? What's shifted?
```

## Example Weekly Analysis Output

Based on the example entries above, Copilot would generate something like:

### Energy Patterns
Your energy dips on days with emotionally complex situations (retro, long planning) and recovers on days with structured activities and peer connection. Tuesday (retro day) and Wednesday (planning) were your lowest energy days. Friday, with peer learning and proactive work, was your highest.

### Recurring Themes
- **Conflict avoidance in the moment** — appeared Monday (Raj email), Wednesday (David's lateness, Priya's tech issue), and is a thread through Tuesday's retro
- **Time management in ceremonies** — planning ran over, retro ran out of time for James
- **Manual work displacing strategic work** — Friday's sprint report realization

### Top 2 Strengths
1. **1:1 coaching** — Marcus and Raj both opened up to you this week
2. **Noticing dynamics** — You consistently saw what was happening (Raj's shutdown, Marcus's growth, Lisa's dominance) even when you didn't always act on it

### Top 2 Growth Areas
1. **Real-time facilitation under pressure** — When emotions rise or time gets tight, you lose structure
2. **Micro-confrontations** — You need to build the muscle of addressing small issues (lateness, tech issues, time overruns) in the moment

### Top 3 Actions for Next Week
1. Set a phone timer for all ceremonies with a 15-min-before-end alarm. When it goes off, stop and prioritize remaining items.
2. Automate the sprint report using Copilot. Spend the saved hour preparing for your Jennifer 1:1 instead.
3. Practice one micro-confrontation per day. Start with low-stakes ones. Track them here.

## Monthly Review Prompt

At the end of each month, paste all 4 weekly analyses and ask:

```
Here are my 4 weekly SM reflection analyses:

[Paste all 4 weekly analyses]

Generate a monthly growth report:

1. What patterns persisted all month? (These are deep habits, not one-offs.)
2. Where did I measurably improve? (Cite specific evidence from the entries.)
3. What's my biggest blind spot that I keep not addressing?
4. How has my SM identity evolved this month?
5. What should my focus areas be for next month?
6. On a scale of 1-10, how intentional was my SM practice this month?
   What would make it a point higher?
```
