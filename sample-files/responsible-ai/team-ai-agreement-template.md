## 1. Purpose

This agreement establishes how our team uses AI tools (Microsoft Copilot, etc.) in our daily work. It ensures we use AI responsibly, protect team members' privacy, and maintain trust.

---

## 2. What We Share with AI

### Always OK (Green Light)
- Aggregated sprint metrics (velocity, burndown, cycle time)
- Generic process descriptions and ceremony formats
- Anonymized retrospective themes
- Industry research questions
- Template generation requests
- General agile coaching questions

### Requires Anonymization First (Yellow Light)
- Team dynamics descriptions (replace names with roles)
- Impediment descriptions (remove client/vendor names)
- Coaching scenario preparation (use fictional names)
- Sprint planning context (remove client-specific details)

### Never Share (Red Light)
- Individual performance data or assessments
- Personal information (health, family, career plans)
- Client-confidential project details
- HR-related conversations or decisions
- Compensation or promotion discussions
- Any data someone has shared in confidence

---

## 3. How We Use AI Output

| Use Case | AI Role | Human Role |
|----------|---------|------------|
| Meeting summaries | Draft the summary | Review, verify accuracy, add nuance |
| Retrospective analysis | Identify patterns and themes | Validate with team, decide actions |
| Coaching preparation | Generate conversation frameworks | Adapt to individual, exercise judgment |
| Impediment research | Research solutions and precedents | Evaluate fit for our context, decide |
| Sprint reporting | Format data into reports | Verify numbers, add narrative context |
| **People decisions** | **NOT USED** | **100% human judgment** |

**Core Rule:** AI output is _input to our judgment_, never _the judgment itself_.

---

## 4. Transparency

- When AI-generated content is shared with the team (e.g., a meeting summary), we disclose that AI was used
- Team members can ask: "Was AI used to create this?" and will always get an honest answer
- AI-generated analysis of team dynamics is shared with the team, not used behind their backs

---

## 5. Individual Rights

- Any team member can request that their personal information NOT be shared with AI tools
- Any team member can ask to see what data about them has been shared with AI
- Opt-out requests are respected without question or judgment
- These rights apply regardless of organizational AI policies

---

## 6. Review Cadence

- This agreement is reviewed every quarter at a retrospective
- Any team member can request a review at any time
- Changes require team consensus
