# AI-Augmented SM Weekly Operating System

**Scrum Master:** You (Velocity Squad)
**Sprint Cadence:** 2 weeks, Wednesday to Tuesday
**Version:** Applies to any sprint week (Week 1 or Week 2)

---

## Purpose

This is your weekly rhythm as an AI-augmented Scrum Master. Every recurring activity is mapped to a time slot, a Copilot prompt (where applicable), required data inputs, and expected outputs. The goal: spend less time on information processing and more time on coaching, facilitation, and strategic work.
