## Engagement Strategy Per Stakeholder

### Jennifer Walsh — Manage Closely (High Power, High Interest)

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **What she cares about** | Program delivery timelines, team morale, risk to AKX project milestones |
| **Key message** | "We have the data to make this case. I need your sponsorship to take it to Richard." |
| **Evidence to present** | 6 missed sprint goals in 5 sprints, velocity decline on Catalyst Crew (42 → 31), David's burnout signals |
| **Ask** | Co-sponsor the business case; introduce you to Richard for the pitch |
| **Format** | 1:1 conversation, followed by shared document review |
| **Risk** | She may want to wait for "the right time" — counter with cost-of-delay data ($7,000/sprint) |

### Richard Thorne — Keep Satisfied (High Power, Low Interest)

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **What he cares about** | Budget efficiency, organizational capability, knowledge management outcomes |
| **Key message** | "One hire at $95K/year recovers $180K/year in lost productivity. Net positive in 6 months." |
| **Evidence to present** | Business case with ROI analysis, comparison to contractor costs, industry benchmarks |
| **Ask** | Approve headcount and budget for 1 mid-level UX designer |
| **Format** | 5-minute executive pitch (see executive-pitch-template.md), followed by business case document |
| **Risk** | May defer to next quarter — prepare cost-of-delay projection showing $28K lost per month of delay |

### Tom Harrison — Keep Informed (Low Power, High Interest)

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **What he cares about** | Insight Engine team getting any design support at all |
| **Key message** | "The proposal includes coverage for Insight Engine. Your team's needs strengthen the case." |
| **Evidence to present** | Current zero-allocation status, features shipped without any design review |
| **Ask** | Document 3-5 examples of Insight Engine features that suffered from no design input |
| **Format** | Quick Slack message + 15-minute chat |
| **Risk** | Low — he's motivated. May over-promise what design support his team would get |

### Ana Martinez & Chris Wong — Keep Informed (Low Power, High Interest)

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **What they care about** | Team velocity, unblocking their backlogs, reducing frustration |
| **Key message** | "I'm building the business case. I need your sprint data and specific examples." |
| **Evidence to present** | Share the draft business case for their review |
| **Ask** | Provide: (1) count of stories blocked on design per sprint, (2) specific examples with business impact, (3) willingness to present their perspective if needed |
| **Format** | SM community of practice meeting + shared document |
| **Risk** | May vent frustration publicly before the formal case is ready — align on timing |

### David Kim — Keep Informed (Low Power, High Interest)

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **What he cares about** | Sustainable workload, quality of his design work, career growth |
| **Key message** | "We're taking action on this. Here's the plan. I need your input but not your labor." |
| **Evidence to present** | The business case approach (don't burden him with building it) |
| **Ask** | (1) Confirm the data points about context-switching overhead, (2) share what "sustainable" would look like to him, (3) be available if leadership wants his perspective |
| **Format** | Private 1:1 — do NOT put him in front of leadership unless he volunteers |
| **Risk** | He may feel exposed or worried about being seen as "complaining" — frame as systemic, not personal |

### HR Team — Monitor / Engage (Medium Power, Medium Interest)

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **What they care about** | Following process, headcount approvals, job description accuracy |
| **Key message** | "We'll have budget approval from Richard. Here's a draft job description. How fast can we move?" |
| **Evidence to present** | Approved business case, job requirements |
| **Ask** | Pre-draft the job requisition so it's ready to post immediately upon approval |
| **Format** | Email with attachments, then a scheduling meeting |
| **Risk** | Standard hiring timeline is 6-8 weeks — ask about expedited options or contractor bridge |

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Fails | Better Approach |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Going directly to Richard without Jennifer | Bypasses the organizational hierarchy; he may defer to her anyway | Build the coalition first |
| Letting David present the case | Puts the burned-out person in a vulnerable position | You present; David confirms data only if comfortable |
| Framing it as "David needs help" | Makes it about one person, not a systemic issue | Frame as organizational capacity gap |
| Presenting without quantified data | Richard is numbers-driven; anecdotes won't move him | Lead with $180K/year cost, 47 story points/month |
| Asking for 2 designers immediately | Bigger ask = harder approval | Ask for 1 now, with a clause to evaluate adding a 2nd in 6 months |
