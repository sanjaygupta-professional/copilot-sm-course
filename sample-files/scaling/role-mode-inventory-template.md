# SM Role Mode Inventory

A tool for mapping your daily activities to the seven Scrum Master role modes, revealing which modes dominate your practice and which are underserved.

---

## The Seven Role Modes

| # | Role Mode | Description | Example Activities |
|---|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Facilitator** | Designing and running meetings, enabling productive conversations | Sprint ceremonies, workshops, decision-making sessions |
| 2 | **Coach** | Developing individuals and team capability through questions and guidance | 1:1s, mentoring, helping team self-organize |
| 3 | **Impediment Remover** | Identifying and eliminating blockers to team flow | Escalating issues, coordinating with other teams, fixing process bottlenecks |
| 4 | **Change Agent** | Driving organizational improvement beyond the team boundary | Process improvement proposals, influencing leadership, challenging status quo |
| 5 | **Servant Leader** | Supporting team needs, shielding from disruption, enabling focus | Backlog admin, environment setup, stakeholder management |
| 6 | **Trainer** | Teaching agile principles, practices, and tools | Workshops, knowledge sharing, onboarding new team members |
| 7 | **Bridge** | Connecting teams, stakeholders, and information across boundaries | Cross-team coordination, dependency management, stakeholder updates |

### Over-Represented Modes

- **Facilitator (32%)** — Above target range. Sprint ceremonies naturally drive this up on ceremony-heavy weeks (Thu/Fri). Consider: are all facilitated sessions necessary? Could the team self-facilitate daily standups?
- **Servant Leader (15%)** — At upper bound. Administrative tasks like board updates and wiki maintenance could be shared with the team.

### Under-Represented Modes

- **Impediment Remover (5%)** — Below target. This week had fewer blockers, but the David Kim dependency and environment access issues suggest organizational impediments that need sustained attention, not just reactive fixes.
- **Coach (12%)** — Below target. Only 4 coaching conversations this week. Consider scheduling more intentional 1:1 coaching time rather than ad-hoc sessions.
- **Change Agent (9%)** — Below target. The business case work is good, but this mode needs more sustained effort to drive the designer capacity issue to resolution.

## How to Use This Template

1. **Track for a full week** — Log every activity with its role mode
2. **Calculate percentages** — Use the summary table format above
3. **Compare to targets** — Identify over/under-represented modes
4. **Set quarterly goals** — Shift 5% from an over-represented mode to an under-represented one
5. **Repeat monthly** — Track your evolution over time
6. **Share with your manager or coach** — Use as a development conversation tool

### Prompt for Copilot Analysis

> *"Here is my SM activity log for this week. Map each activity to the 7 SM role modes (facilitator, coach, impediment remover, change agent, servant leader, trainer, bridge). Calculate time distribution percentages. Identify which modes are over/under-represented compared to a mature Scrum Master profile, and suggest 3 specific shifts I could make next week."*
