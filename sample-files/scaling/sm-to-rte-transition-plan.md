# SM to RTE Transition Plan — 90-Day Template

A structured plan for a Scrum Master transitioning into a Release Train Engineer or program-level coordination role, based on the Horizon Program context.

---

## Current State Assessment

| Dimension | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Current Role** | Scrum Master, Velocity Squad (Core Platform) |
| **Program** | Horizon Program — 4 teams, AKX project |
| **Teams in Scope** | Velocity Squad, Catalyst Crew, Nexus Team, Insight Engine |
| **Current RTE** | Jennifer Walsh (VP Engineering) — holding both VP and RTE duties |
| **Transition Trigger** | Jennifer needs to focus on VP responsibilities; program needs dedicated coordination |

| Risk | Mitigation |

## Phase 2: Establish Program Cadences (Days 31-60)

**Goal:** Create the rhythms and forums that enable cross-team coordination. Start adding value visibly.

### Week 5-6: Launch Core Cadences

| Cadence | Frequency | Participants | Purpose |
|---------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Scrum of Scrums** | 2x/week (Tue, Thu) | SMs + you | Cross-team blockers, dependency coordination |
| **PO Sync** | Weekly (Wed) | POs + you | Backlog alignment, priority conflicts, shared roadmap |
| **Program Standup** | Weekly (Mon) | Team leads + you | High-level status, upcoming coordination needs |
| **Inspect & Adapt** | Every 3 sprints | All teams | Program-level retrospective and improvement planning |

### Week 7-8: Build Program Visibility

| Action | Details | Done? |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Launch program board | Visualize cross-team work, dependencies, and milestones | [ ] |
| Create program sprint report | Consolidated view of all 4 teams (RAG status, metrics, risks) | [ ] |
| Establish escalation path | Clear process for when team-level impediments need program attention | [ ] |
| Begin addressing quick wins | Execute 1-2 from the Phase 1 list | [ ] |
| Coach Insight Engine | Begin helping the team without an SM establish basic Scrum practices | [ ] |

### Phase 2 Deliverables

- [ ] All program cadences running with consistent attendance
- [ ] Program board live and updated
- [ ] First program sprint report published
- [ ] Escalation process documented and communicated
- [ ] Insight Engine has basic Scrum ceremonies established

### Phase 2 Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Cadence fatigue — too many meetings | Start lean. Each meeting must prove its value or get cut. |
| SMs feel micromanaged | Position yourself as serving the SMs, not supervising them. Ask: "What do you need from me?" |
| Insight Engine resistance | They've operated without an SM. Go slow. Offer help, don't impose structure. |

## What Changes, What Stays, What's New

### What Changes

| From (SM) | To (RTE) |
|-----------|----------|
| Serve 1 team | Serve 4 teams and their SMs |
| Facilitate team ceremonies | Facilitate program-level events |
| Remove team impediments | Remove organizational impediments |
| Coach team members | Coach Scrum Masters |
| Report to team stakeholders | Report to program stakeholders and leadership |
| Optimize team flow | Optimize cross-team flow and dependencies |

### What Stays the Same

- Servant leadership mindset
- Facilitation skills (applied at larger scale)
- Data-driven approach to improvement
- Relentless focus on removing impediments
- Coaching stance over directing

### New Skills Needed

| Skill | Why | How to Develop |
|-------|-----|----------------|
| **Program-level facilitation** | PI Planning, Inspect & Adapt events | SAFe RTE course, practice with smaller events |
| **Organizational influence** | Driving change without authority across teams | Stakeholder mapping, coalition building, executive communication |
| **Cross-team dependency management** | Preventing integration failures | Dependency mapping tools, release planning techniques |
| **Executive communication** | Reporting to VP/C-level stakeholders | Practice 5-minute pitch format, data-driven storytelling |
| **Coaching coaches** | Developing other SMs rather than doing the work | Meta-coaching skills, peer coaching circles |

### What to Delegate

| Activity | Delegate To | Timeline |
|----------|------------|----------|
| Velocity Squad daily standup | New SM or team self-facilitation | Day 1-15 |
| Velocity Squad sprint planning | New SM + Raj (PO) | Day 15-30 |
| Velocity Squad retro facilitation | New SM | Day 30-45 |
| Velocity Squad impediment log | New SM | Day 15-30 |
| Team-level metrics tracking | New SM or automate | Day 30-45 |

## Risk Factors for the Transition

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| No successor identified for Velocity Squad SM | Medium | High | Begin recruiting/identifying candidate in Phase 1 |
| Jennifer doesn't fully hand off RTE duties | Medium | Medium | Explicit handoff checklist with dates; weekly check-in on transfer |
| Other SMs don't accept your authority | Low | High | Build trust through service, not directives. Earn authority. |
| Insight Engine team resists any process | High | Medium | Start with what they value (e.g., unblocking them), not ceremonies |
| Trying to do both SM and RTE roles | High | High | Hard deadline: fully transition SM duties by Day 45 |
| Scope creep from Jennifer's VP responsibilities | Medium | Medium | Clear RTE role boundaries documented and agreed |
