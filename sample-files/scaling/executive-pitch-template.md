# Executive Pitch: Resolving the Design Bottleneck in the Horizon Program

**Audience:** Richard Thorne, Chief Knowledge Officer
**Duration:** 5 minutes (with 5 minutes for Q&A)
**Setting:** Scheduled 1:1 or end of program review
**Sponsor:** Jennifer Walsh, VP / RTE
**Prepared By:** You (Scrum Master / Program Coordinator)

---

## Pitch Structure Overview

| Segment | Duration | Purpose |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Hook | 15 seconds | Create urgency with one compelling number |
| Problem | 60 seconds | Define the bottleneck with human context |
| Data | 60 seconds | Quantify the cost — make inaction expensive |
| Proposal | 60 seconds | Present the solution with clear ROI |
| Ask | 30 seconds | Specific, actionable request |
| Close | 15 seconds | Next steps and timeline |
| **Total** | **~4 minutes** | Leaves buffer for questions |

---

## The Pitch (Script)

### HOOK — 15 seconds

> "Richard, we're losing 47 story points per month across the Horizon Program to a problem that has a clear, cost-effective fix. I'd like to walk you through it in five minutes."

**Why this works:** Starts with a quantified problem, not a complaint. Respects his time by setting a duration.

---

### PROBLEM — 60 seconds

> "When the Horizon Program started, one designer was enough. We've grown to four teams, but David Kim is still the only designer — and he's spread across three of them. The fourth team, Insight Engine, has no design support at all.
>
> Here's what that looks like in practice: on any given day, two teams are waiting for David. Stories sit blocked for an average of 4.2 days. Sprint goals get missed because design work isn't ready. And David himself has told us the pace is unsustainable — he's context-switching across three codebases, three backlogs, and three sets of stakeholders."

**Supporting visual** (if screen available):

```
TEAM ALLOCATION — CURRENT STATE

Velocity Squad (Core Platform)  ──┐
Catalyst Crew (Search)          ──┼── David Kim (1 designer, 100%)
Nexus Team (Mobile)             ──┘
Insight Engine (Analytics)      ──── No designer allocated
```

---

### DATA — 60 seconds

> "Let me share three numbers that frame the cost of this bottleneck."

| Metric | Value | What It Means |
|--------|-------|---------------|
| **47 story points blocked/month** | Across 3 teams, Sprints 43-47 | Nearly a full team's sprint capacity sitting idle waiting for design |
| **6 sprint goals missed** | In the last 5 sprints due to design delays | Predictability is suffering — we can't reliably commit to stakeholders |
| **$180,000/year** | Estimated cost of lost productivity | Based on blended team cost of $150/hour x blocked capacity |

> "To put it another way: we have $1.2 million in annual team cost across these three teams, and roughly 15% of their capacity is wasted waiting for a single shared resource. That's the equivalent of paying for 4 teams but getting the output of 3.4."

**Trend line** (if screen available):

| Sprint | Catalyst Crew Velocity | Design-Blocked Stories |
|--------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Sprint 42 | 42 points | 2 |
| Sprint 43 | 38 points | 3 |
| Sprint 44 | 36 points | 4 |
| Sprint 45 | 34 points | 5 |
| Sprint 46 | 33 points | 4 |
| Sprint 47 | 31 points | 5 |

> "Catalyst Crew has lost 26% of their velocity over six sprints. The design bottleneck isn't the only factor, but it's the biggest controllable one."

---

### PROPOSAL — 60 seconds

> "We're proposing one additional mid-level UX designer. Not two — just one. Here's the math."

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Annual cost of 1 mid-level UX designer | $95,000 |
| Annual cost of lost productivity (current state) | $180,000 |
| **Net benefit in Year 1** | **$85,000+** |
| Break-even point | ~6 months after full onboarding |

> "With two designers, we can assign one per two teams. David takes Velocity Squad and Catalyst Crew — teams he already knows well. The new hire takes Nexus and Insight Engine. Context-switching drops dramatically. Wait times drop from 4+ days to under 1 day."

**Proposed state:**

```
TEAM ALLOCATION — PROPOSED STATE

Velocity Squad (Core Platform)  ──┐
                                  ├── David Kim (Senior Designer)
Catalyst Crew (Search)          ──┘

Nexus Team (Mobile)             ──┐
                                  ├── New Hire (Mid-Level Designer)
Insight Engine (Analytics)      ──┘
```

> "David also becomes a mentor to the new hire, which builds organizational resilience. We stop being one resignation away from a program-wide design crisis."

---

### ASK — 30 seconds

> "I'm asking for two things:
>
> 1. **Headcount approval** for one mid-level UX designer, budget of $95,000/year
> 2. **A decision by Sprint 48** — every sprint of delay costs approximately $7,000 in lost team productivity
>
> Jennifer supports this proposal. HR has confirmed they can post the requisition within 48 hours of approval. We've pre-drafted the job description."

---

### CLOSE — 15 seconds

> "I'll send you the full business case document today. It has the detailed data, implementation timeline, and risk assessment. Happy to answer any questions now, or schedule a follow-up if you'd prefer to review the document first."

## Anticipated Questions & Responses

| Question | Response |
|----------|----------|
| "Can't we use contractors instead?" | "We explored that. Contractors cost $120-150/hour ($250K-$310K/year), take 2-4 weeks to onboard each sprint, and don't build institutional knowledge. A full-time hire at $95K is 60-70% cheaper and more effective long-term. We could use a contractor as a bridge while we hire." |
| "Can David just prioritize better?" | "David is already triaging ruthlessly. The issue isn't prioritization — it's physics. One person cannot do 3 people's work. He's already working evenings to keep up, which is a retention risk." |
| "What if we reorganize teams to reduce design needs?" | "Some teams could defer design-heavy work, but that means deferring the highest-value features. Our POs have confirmed the design-heavy items are also the highest-priority items." |
| "Why not wait until next quarter's budget cycle?" | "Four more sprints at $7,000/sprint = $28,000 in additional lost productivity. Plus the 6-8 week hiring timeline means the new designer wouldn't start until 4-5 months from now even if we approve today." |
| "How do we know one designer is enough?" | "The data shows 1 designer per 2 teams is the industry standard for programs at our scale. We recommend evaluating after 3 months and making the case for a second hire if the data supports it." |
| "What's the risk of NOT acting?" | "Three risks: (1) David burns out and leaves — then we have zero design capacity for 2-3 months, (2) Catalyst Crew velocity continues declining, jeopardizing Q1 commitments, (3) Insight Engine ships with no design review, creating UX debt." |
