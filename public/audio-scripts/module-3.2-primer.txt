Let me ask you something: when was the last time you celebrated your team's wins in a way that felt genuinely memorable? Not just "great sprint everyone" in a Teams message — but something that acknowledged the work, marked the milestone, made people feel seen?

Scrum Masters underinvest in celebration. It's understandable — there's always something to fix, something to improve, something to escalate. But teams that feel genuinely recognised perform better, collaborate more willingly, and stay longer. And you now have the ability to create beautiful, custom visuals that make celebration feel real.

Module 3.2 is about building a visual library for your regular SM needs — the four categories that come up sprint after sprint. Sprint review visuals that engage stakeholders rather than bore them. Retrospective facilitation aids that make sessions feel different and creative. Team celebration graphics that honour milestones with something better than a group emoji. And process documentation visuals that make your working agreements and Scrum event guidelines clear and approachable.

You'll practise each category with specific, reusable prompt patterns. And crucially, you'll build a prompt library — a collection of your successful image prompts — so you're not reinventing the approach every time.

The goal by the end of this module isn't just skill — it's a ready-to-use visual toolkit that will serve you for the rest of this course and beyond.
