When those two dimensions fall together, the team is telling you something important that they're not saying out loud. Your job is to hear it before it becomes a retention problem or a team breakdown.

And more importantly — what's one thing you could do this sprint that would have a measurable positive effect on that dimension? Not a big programme. One thing.

Your action: if you don't currently run team health surveys, start simple. Five dimensions, five-point scale, every sprint. That's it.
