There's a fundamental challenge in Scrum Master development that this module addresses directly: the situations that matter most — the heated retrospective, the resistant stakeholder, the team member in crisis — are the hardest to practise safely. You can't rehearse a conflict mediation with your actual team. You can't rewind a coaching conversation that went sideways and try a different approach.

Until now.

This module gives you a coaching gym. A space where Copilot plays any team member, with any level of difficulty, and then steps out of character to give you honest feedback on your approach. It's not a replacement for real experience — nothing is. But it's the most effective practice environment that exists outside of live situations.

You'll work with the difficulty ladder: the same scenario at escalating challenge levels. Marcus at difficulty level one is genuinely trying to engage. Marcus at difficulty level three says "everything's fine" and deflects every question. Marcus at difficulty level five actively resists your attempts to create space for him. Each level teaches you something different about your coaching instincts and where they break down.

You'll also practise multi-party simulations — managing competing needs in real time, the way your actual ceremonies sometimes feel. And you'll use AI-assisted reflective journaling to identify patterns in your own practice that are hard to see from inside your own perspective.

This module asks you to engage genuinely. Type what you'd actually say. The value comes from the feedback, not from crafting the perfect prompt.

Let's get to work on your craft.
