You'll start with conflict pattern recognition — learning to read the signals in your ceremony data that surface tensions before they're named. Then you'll use Co-pilot to prepare for difficult conversations with psychological depth: understanding what's driving the tension beneath the surface, not just what's being said on top of it.

The centrepiece of this module is persona-based roleplay at escalating difficulty levels. You'll practise with a simulation that doesn't make it easy for you — a team member who deflects, a conversation that gets uncomfortable, a conflict that has two legitimate sides. And you'll get honest feedback on your approach.

By the end of this module, you'll have a framework for de-escalation, specific language patterns for the hardest moments, and a practice record of having had the difficult conversations before you have them for real.
