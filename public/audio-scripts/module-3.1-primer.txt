This module introduces you to DALL-E 3 image generation through Co-pilot — and if you've never used AI to create visuals before, prepare to be a little amazed. You describe what you want in plain English, and Co-pilot generates a custom image. No design software. No stock photo subscriptions. No design skills required.

Before you dive in, though, there are three things worth knowing. What DALL-E 3 is genuinely great at — illustrations, concept visuals, abstract graphics, celebration graphics for team milestones. What it's less suited for — precise diagrams with specific text labels, photos of real people, technically exact flowcharts. And how to write a prompt that actually produces what you want, because image prompting has its own specific patterns that are different from text prompting.

The principle is style plus subject plus composition plus mood. Get those four elements in your prompt and the outputs become dramatically more useful and consistent.

You'll practise generating sprint review slide backgrounds, celebration graphics, and process visuals — the types of images that show up most in SM work. And you'll learn to iterate: when the first output isn't quite right, how to refine the prompt to get closer.
