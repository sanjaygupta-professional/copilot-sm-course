You've just developed one of the most sophisticated meta-skills in the Scrum Master toolkit: knowing not just what to do, but which version of yourself to be in a given situation.

The diagnostic habit to build: before every significant SM interaction — a one-to-one, a ceremony, a difficult conversation — ask yourself which hat this situation actually requires. Not which hat I feel like wearing or which I'm best at. Which one does this situation need?

Here's a reflection: when you tagged your actual week by role mode, what was the distribution? Most SMs find they're over-indexed on facilitator and impediment remover, and under-indexed on coach and change agent. If that's true for you, what does that mean for the team members who need coaching? Are they getting what they need?

Your action: identify one situation this sprint where you've been wearing the wrong hat — likely applying facilitation logic where coaching logic is needed, or handling something as an impediment where it actually calls for a change agent approach. Consciously put on the right hat and handle it differently. Notice what the person or team does differently in response. That experiment teaches you more about role fluency than any framework can.
