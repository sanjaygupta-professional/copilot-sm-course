Here's a challenge for you: think about the last ceremony you facilitated that didn't go quite the way you hoped. Maybe sprint planning ran long. Maybe the retrospective felt flat. Maybe voices got lost. Now — how long before that ceremony did you start preparing? And when you prepared, was it structured, or was it mostly "I'll figure it out in the room"?

Level 2 starts here, and it starts with a significant upgrade to how you show up for your ceremonies.

Copilot as a facilitation partner changes the preparation game entirely. Instead of arriving with a rough agenda, you arrive with a facilitation guide — complete with timebox allocations, specific questions to ask at each stage, signals to watch for that indicate the team is about to overcommit or shut down, and pre-planned interventions for when things go off track.

This module covers all four Scrum ceremonies. Sprint planning, with its capacity maths and its tendency to run over time. The Daily Scrum, with its 25-minute creep and its quiet voices. The Sprint Review, where you need to engage stakeholders while the team demonstrates their work. And the Retrospective — especially the retrospective — where fresh formats and genuine psychological safety make the difference between action that sticks and conversation that doesn't.

The most valuable skill you'll build here isn't the prompting — it's the habit of thinking through your ceremonies before they happen, systematically and with intention. Copilot is the forcing function for that habit.

Your facilitation is about to get noticeably more deliberate. Let's build it.
