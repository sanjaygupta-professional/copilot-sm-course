# Ceremony Automation Chain

## When to Use
Use this 4-step chain to automate the entire ceremony lifecycle: preparation, real-time support, post-processing, and follow-up.

---

## Step 1: Pre-Ceremony (Run 1 hour before)

```
I have a [CEREMONY TYPE] in 1 hour for the Velocity Squad (Sprint [X]).

AVAILABLE DATA:
[Attach or reference: backlog, previous retro actions, velocity data, impediment log]

Generate:
1. AGENDA with timing for a [DURATION]-minute session
2. KEY DATA POINTS to reference during the session (3-5 metrics or facts)
3. WATCH-FORS: 2-3 team dynamics or topics that may surface
4. PARKING LOT PREP: Topics from last sprint that were deferred

Format as a single-page facilitation brief I can have open during the meeting.
```

## Step 2: During Ceremony (Note-Taking Template)

Copy this structure into your note-taking app. Fill in during the ceremony:


## Step 3: Post-Ceremony (Run within 30 min after)

```
Process these ceremony notes into actionable outputs.

RAW NOTES:
[Paste your notes from Step 2]

Generate:
1. MEETING SUMMARY (5-7 sentences, suitable for sharing in team Slack channel)
2. ACTION ITEMS formatted as:
   - [ ] [Action] — @[Owner] — Due: [Date]
3. DECISIONS LOG: List each decision with brief rationale
4. RISKS: Any risks raised, with suggested mitigation
5. SM FOLLOW-UP: What I need to do before next ceremony

Tone: Clear, concise, action-oriented. No fluff.
```

## Step 4: Follow-Up (Run 3 days after)

```
Generate follow-up messages for these action items from our [CEREMONY TYPE]:

ACTION ITEMS:
[Paste action items from Step 3]

For each action item, generate a brief, friendly Teams/Slack message to the owner:
- Remind them of the action and due date
- Ask if they need any help or if there are blockers
- Keep it under 3 sentences
- Tone: supportive, not nagging

Also flag any items that are past due with a slightly more direct message.
```

| Activity | Manual | With Chain | Savings |
|----------|--------|------------|---------|
| Pre-ceremony prep | 20 min | 5 min | 15 min |
