# Reflective Journal Template

## When to Use
Use daily (5 minutes) or weekly (15 minutes) to develop reflective practice as a Scrum Master. Copilot serves as your thinking partner, helping you see patterns you might miss.

## Daily Reflection (5 min)

```
Here's what happened in my SM work today:

1. [Event/interaction 1]
2. [Event/interaction 2]
3. [Event/interaction 3]

For each event:
- What did I do well?
- What would I do differently with hindsight?
- What does this tell me about my SM practice?

Also: Is there anything I'm avoiding or procrastinating on? Name it.
```

## Weekly Pattern Review (15 min, end of week)

```
Here are my daily reflections from this week:

[Paste 5 daily entries]

Analyze my week as a Scrum Master:

1. PATTERNS: What themes or recurring situations do you notice?
2. ENERGY: When was I most/least energized? What does that signal?
3. GROWTH: Where am I improving compared to last week/month?
4. BLIND SPOTS: What might I be missing or avoiding?
5. NEXT WEEK FOCUS: Based on this week's patterns, what ONE thing should I practice or prioritize next week?

Be direct and honest. I want coaching, not compliments.
```

## Monthly Practice Assessment (30 min, end of month)

```
Here are my weekly reviews from this month:

[Paste 4 weekly summaries]

Give me a monthly practice assessment:

1. COMPETENCY GROWTH: Which SM competencies am I developing? Which are stagnant?
2. IMPACT: What tangible difference am I making for my team this month?
3. RELATIONSHIPS: How are my key relationships (team, PO, management) evolving?
4. BLIND SPOTS: What pattern have I mentioned multiple times without acting on?
5. CHALLENGE: Give me one stretch goal for next month that pushes me outside my comfort zone.

Frame this as a coaching conversation, not a performance review.
```
