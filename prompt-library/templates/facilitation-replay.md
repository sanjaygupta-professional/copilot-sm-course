# Facilitation Replay

## When to Use
Use after any ceremony to get AI feedback on your facilitation decisions. This is deliberate practice — reviewing what happened and identifying what you'd do differently.

## The Prompt

```
I just facilitated a [CEREMONY TYPE] for my team. Help me replay and learn.

WHAT HAPPENED:
[Describe the ceremony in 5-10 bullet points. Include what you did, how people reacted, key moments, and the outcome.]

WHAT I WAS TRYING TO DO:
[Your facilitation goals for this session]

MOMENTS I'M UNSURE ABOUT:
1. [Describe a moment where you weren't sure you handled it well]
2. [Another moment]

Analyze my facilitation:
1. WHAT WORKED: Specific moments where my facilitation was effective, and why
2. MISSED OPPORTUNITIES: Moments where a different intervention would have produced a better outcome. Be specific — tell me what I should have said or done.
3. PATTERN CHECK: Based on this one ceremony, do you see any patterns in my facilitation style that I should be aware of? (e.g., "You tend to rescue people from silence instead of letting it work")
4. ONE THING TO TRY NEXT TIME: A single, specific facilitation technique I should practice in my next ceremony
```

## Example

```
I just facilitated a retrospective for the Velocity Squad. Help me replay and learn.

WHAT HAPPENED:
- Used the "Glad/Sad/Mad" format
- Lisa immediately dominated the "Mad" section with a 3-minute monologue about design blockers
- Raj was quiet the entire time, only spoke when I directly asked him
- Marcus said "I agree with Lisa" on every topic instead of sharing his own perspective
- Priya (remote) had her camera off and was hard to read
- James raised a valid point about test coverage but it got buried
- We generated 6 action items but I'm not confident we picked the right ones
- The session ran 10 minutes over

WHAT I WAS TRYING TO DO:
- Get honest feedback from everyone, not just the loud voices
- Address the design bottleneck without making David feel attacked
- Keep it under 90 minutes

MOMENTS I'M UNSURE ABOUT:
1. When Lisa started her monologue, I let her finish (3 minutes). Should I have redirected sooner?
2. I asked Raj "What do you think?" directly. He gave a short answer. Should I have approached differently?

Analyze my facilitation.
```
