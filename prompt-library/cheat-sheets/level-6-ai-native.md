# Level 6 Cheat Sheet: AI-Native SM Mastery

---

## Agents & Automation (Module 6.1)

### Time-Sink Audit
```
Here's my weekly SM schedule:
[List activities with time estimates]
For each: rate automatable (Fully/Partially/Not at all), suggest Copilot approach, estimate time savings.
Calculate total weekly savings.
```

### One-Prompt Sprint Report
```
Generate a sprint report from: [attach velocity CSV + impediment log + standup notes]
Three versions: Team (learning), Management (reliability), Executive (business outcomes, no jargon).
Each under 200 words.
```

### Ceremony Automation Chain
```
Step 1 (Pre): Generate agenda from [backlog + retro actions + capacity]
Step 2 (During): [use structured note template]
Step 3 (Post): Process notes into [summary + actions + decisions + risks]
Step 4 (Follow-up): Generate reminder messages for [action owners]
```

### Human Judgment Boundary
```
For this automated workflow: [description]
Classify each output:
- AUTO-DO: Agent executes without human review
- RECOMMEND: Agent suggests, human decides
- NEVER-TOUCH: Human only, AI not involved
Explain the reasoning for each classification.
```

## Coaching Simulator (Module 6.2)

### Coaching Gym
```
Play [person description] at [easy/medium/hard] difficulty.
Context: [situation]. Stay in character. After I say "break character":
Rate me on: empathy, questioning, patience, non-judgment, action-orientation (1-5 each).
Tell me: what worked, what a master coach would do differently, one thing to try next time.
```

### Difficulty Ladder
```
Same scenario, 3 rounds:
Round 1 (Easy): [Person] is receptive and shares openly
Round 2 (Medium): [Person] deflects, says "I'm fine"
Round 3 (Hard): [Person] is defensive, pushes back

I'll practice each round. Give feedback after each.
```

### Multi-Party Simulation
```
Simulate a retro where you play 3 people simultaneously:
- [Person A]: [motivation and emotional state]
- [Person B]: [motivation and emotional state]
- [Person C]: [motivation and emotional state]
I'll facilitate. Respond as each character would react to my facilitation moves.
Signal when someone is about to disengage or escalate.
```

### Facilitation Replay
```
I just facilitated [ceremony type]. Here's what happened:
[5-10 bullet points]
Analyze: What worked, missed opportunities, patterns in my style, one technique to practice next time.
```

## Building Practice (Module 6.3)

### Weekly Reflection
```
My SM week: [3-5 bullet points of what happened]
What patterns do you see? Where am I growing? What should I focus on next week?
Be direct — coaching, not compliments.
```

### 90-Day Plan Check-In
```
I'm on Day [X] of my 90-day AI adoption plan.
Month [1/2/3] goal: [description]
Progress so far: [what I've done]
Am I on track? What should I adjust? Any risks to flag?
```

### Competency Self-Assessment
```
Rate me on the AI-Enabled SM Competency Model based on our conversations:
1. AI Tool Proficiency
2. Advanced Facilitation with AI
3. Data-Driven Decision Making
4. Scaling & Influence
5. AI Coaching & Automation
6. Responsible AI Leadership
For each: current level (1-5), evidence, growth suggestion.
```
